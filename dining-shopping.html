Village Dining & Boutiques

Stroll tree-lined brick pathways through Southampton Village, home to historic bakeries, coastal seafood institutions, and refined storefronts along Main Street and Jobs Lane.

Must-Visit Culinary & Shopping Stops

Bakery Legend

Tate's Bake Shop

The iconic original home of nationally acclaimed thin, crisp chocolate chip cookies, pies, and fresh fruit tarts on North Sea Road.

Italian Cafe

Sant Ambroeus

A sophisticated Milanese espresso bar and restaurant on Main Street, famous for artisanal gelato, fresh brioche, and patio people-watching.

Boutique Strolls

Jobs Lane Boutiques

Historic pedestrian corridor lined with indie clothing boutiques, coastal home design stores, and fine art galleries ending at Agawam Park.

Local Dining Etiquette & Timing

Tips for planning meals during busy Hamptons weekends:

  • Dinner Reservations: Book 1–2 weeks in advance on OpenTable or Resy for prime Friday and Saturday evening slots.
  • Casual Lunch: Many spots along Main Street offer grab-and-go sandwich lunches ideal for taking to the beach.
  • Off-Peak Hours: Visiting bakeries like Tate's before 10:00 AM avoids long morning pastry queues.
