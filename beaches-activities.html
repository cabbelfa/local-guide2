Beaches, Nature & Culture

Whether lounging along the rolling Atlantic surf or wandering through iconic East End gallery halls, Southampton balances outdoor beauty with deep creative heritage.

Top Coastal & Cultural Spots

Premier Beach

Coopers Beach

Consistently ranked among the top public beaches in the country. Known for pristine white quartz sand, historic Meadow Lane mansion views, full bathhouses, and umbrella rentals.

Art & Heritage

Parrish Art Museum

An architectural masterpiece set in Water Mill, highlighting artists who were inspired by the East End's unique natural coastal light, including William Merritt Chase and Fairfield Porter.

Outdoor Leisure

Gin Lane Cycling Loop

A classic, flat 6-mile coastal bike route taking riders past historic shingle-style summer cottages, Southampton Bathing Corporation, and scenic Lake Agawam.

Beach Regulations & Daily Parking

Planning a beach day requires a little preparation during peak seasonal months:

  • Daily Passes: Non-resident parking passes are sold on a first-come, first-served basis at the Coopers Beach entrance gate.
  • Lifeguards: Main lifeguard stations operate daily from 10:00 AM to 5:00 PM between Memorial Day and Labor Day.
  • Walk & Bike Access: Pedestrians and cyclists can access the beaches freely without vehicle parking permits.
