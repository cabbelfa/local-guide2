Visitor Transit Tips

Southampton is easily accessible for weekend travelers arriving without a personal car:

  • Long Island Rail Road (LIRR): The Montauk Branch drops off directly at the Southampton Village station.
  • Hampton Jitney: Daily express coach buses connect Manhattan directly to Southampton Main Street.
  • Getting Around: The village core is highly walkable, with seasonal bike rentals available near the station.
